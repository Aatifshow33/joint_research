# The Monthly Money Playbook

> One rule above all: **the saving is the strategy.** The allocation below just
> parks your savings on a sensible risk spectrum so it compounds without any
> single failure wiping you out. Automate it, add money every month, never sell
> the core. That's the whole game.

---

## Phase 0 — The gate (do this BEFORE investing a cent)

**Build an emergency fund: 3–6 months of basic expenses, in a high-yield
savings account or T-bills (~4–5%, genuinely safe).**

Until that account is full, **100% of your spare money goes there.** This is
non-negotiable — it's what lets you stay invested through a bad month instead of
panic-selling at the worst time. No emergency fund = no investing yet.

---

## Phase 1 — The monthly split (once the emergency fund is full)

Every payday, split the money you can invest (after basic needs) like this:

| Bucket | % | Where it goes | Risk |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Core** | **70%** | Broad index fund — VTI or VOO (or a target-date fund) | Low, proven |
| **Yield** | **15%** | Stablecoin lending (USDC) + funding-rate arb | Low-but-real |
| **Crypto** | **10%** | BTC/ETH, bought monthly, held — never traded | High |
| **Reserve** | **5%** | Cash kept aside for dips / opportunities | None |

**At $500/month:** $350 core · $75 yield · $50 crypto · $25 reserve
**At $2,000/month:** $1,400 core · $300 yield · $200 crypto · $100 reserve

---

## The payday checklist (literally what to do)

1. Pay basic needs.
2. Top up emergency fund if it isn't full. (If not full, stop here.)
3. Buy the **Core** index fund — set this to **auto-invest** so it's not a decision.
4. Move the **Yield** slice to stablecoin lending / funding-arb.
5. Buy the **Crypto** slice (same dollar amount every month — that's the point).
6. Leave the **Reserve** in cash.
7. Reinvest any dividends/yield. **Do not sell anything.**

---

## Guardrails (the don'ts that keep you safe)

- **Never** put more than ~10–15% of your total in crypto/arb combined.
- **Never** use high leverage on the arb legs — a price wick can liquidate a leg
  and break the hedge. Keep it low so that can't happen.
- **Never** sell the core because the market dropped — that's when you keep buying.
- **Never** touch the emergency fund except for a real emergency.
- **Never** believe the word "guaranteed" about anything except T-bills/HYSA.

---

## Once a year

- **Rebalance** back to the 70/15/10/5 split (sell a little of what grew most,
  buy what lagged — inside tax-advantaged accounts where possible).
- **Raise the contribution** if your income went up. The single biggest lever on
  your end is putting in more — a raise or side income beats any strategy tweak.

---

## Why this and not "the one perfect trade"

Because at a small base with a high savings rate, the math is dominated by how
much you put in and how consistently — not by squeezing an extra few % out of a
clever strategy. This setup **can't go to zero**, needs **no expertise**, and
**beats most professional traders** over time. The exciting stuff (funding-arb,
prediction-arb, crypto) is a small, controlled slice — seasoning, not the meal.
