# My Monthly Money Playbook — $500/month

> One rule above all: **the saving is the strategy.** $500/month, consistently,
> quietly becomes six figures over a decade. The split below just parks it on a
> sensible risk spectrum so nothing can wipe me out. Automate it, add money every
> month, never sell the core.

**My situation:** income from Handshake AI (1099 / self-employed). Goal: build
wealth steadily with $500/month. Not chasing a quick flip.

---

## What $500/month builds (~10% blended)

| Time | I put in | Balance | From returns |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 year | $6,200 | ~$6,600 | 5% |
| 3 years | $18,200 | ~$21,300 | 15% |
| 5 years | $30,200 | ~$39,400 | 23% |
| 10 years | $60,200 | ~$104,000 | 42% |

Early on it's mostly my own savings; by year 10 the market has made me ~$44k.
That's compounding taking over — it only shows up with time + consistency.

---

## The order I do it each month

**0. Taxes first (separate, on top of the $500).**
Set aside **~25–30% of every Handshake AI payment** into its own savings account
*before* anything else. 1099 income has no withholding — the IRS wants quarterly
estimated payments. Skipping this = a tax bill I can't pay. Non-negotiable.

**1. Phase 0 — fill the emergency fund.**
Until I have **3–6 months of expenses** in a high-yield savings account, the
**entire $500/month goes there.** No investing yet. This is what lets me stay
invested through a bad month instead of panic-selling.

**2. Phase 1 — once the emergency fund is full, split the $500:**

| Bucket | Amount | Where | Risk |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **Core** | **$350** | Index fund (VTI/VOO) inside my **Roth IRA** | Low, proven |
| **Yield** | **$75** | Stablecoin lending (USDC) / funding-arb | Low-but-real |
| **Crypto** | **$50** | BTC/ETH, bought monthly, held — never traded | High |
| **Reserve** | **$25** | Cash for dips / opportunities | None |

---

## Accounts to open (once, online, ~an afternoon)

1. **HYSA** — Ally / Marcus / SoFi (~4%). Two of them: one for taxes, one for the
   emergency fund.
2. **Roth IRA** at Fidelity / Vanguard / Schwab — holds the index fund, grows
   **tax-free** (I qualify: Handshake AI is earned income). ~$7k/yr limit.
3. **Solo 401(k)** (self-employed) — only needed if I later raise contributions
   above the Roth's $7k/yr. Shelters much more.
4. **Crypto:** Coinbase / Kraken for BTC/ETH; Hyperliquid / dYdX for funding-arb.

---

## The payday checklist

1. Pay basic needs.
2. Move **25–30%** of Handshake income to the **tax** HYSA.
3. If emergency fund isn't full → send the **$500** there and **stop**.
4. If it is full → **auto-invest $350** into the index fund (Roth), move **$75**
   to yield, buy **$50** BTC/ETH, leave **$25** cash.
5. Reinvest all dividends/yield. **Do not sell anything.**

---

## Guardrails

- Never more than ~10–15% of total in crypto/arb combined.
- Never high leverage on arb legs (a price wick can liquidate a leg).
- Never sell the core because the market dropped — keep buying.
- Never touch the emergency fund except a real emergency.
- "Guaranteed" only applies to T-bills / HYSA. Nothing else.

---

## Once a year

- Rebalance back to 70/15/10/5.
- Raise the $500 if income grows — more contributions is the biggest lever.
- One session with a CPA for the self-employment taxes + Solo 401(k) is worth it.
